# explaining-questions

Attach a screenshot or paste the question text with answer choices.

## Steps

**1. Restate** the question clearly in one or two sentences.

**2. Identify the domain and skill** (e.g., "Math — Algebra: linear equations" or "R&W — Craft and Structure: transitions").

**3. Explain the correct answer:**
- **Reading/Writing**: Eliminate each wrong answer and state *why* it fails, then confirm why the correct choice works.
- **Math**: Show every algebraic or logical step. Use shortcuts where applicable (e.g., Vieta's formulas for sum/product of roots; "increased by X%" = ×(1 + X/100)).

**4. Name the pattern** the student should remember:
- "Transitions: map what comes before/after the blank, then eliminate."
- "Percentage change 'by X%' means multiply by (1 + X/100); 'to X%' means the result is X%."
- "Sum of roots of ax² + bx + c = 0 is −b/a."

**5. Give one similar practice problem** to try right now. Put the answer after a spoiler break.

Keep explanations short and concrete. No jargon. Plain arithmetic when possible.
